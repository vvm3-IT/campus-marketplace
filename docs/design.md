# Brand & design direction

## Reusing Brand Studio
The existing [Quiet Reps Brand Studio](https://github.com/vvm3-IT/is117-brand-studio) provides the visual foundation. Its [shared CSS tokens](https://github.com/vvm3-IT/is117-brand-studio/blob/main/public/styles.css) and [live style guide source](https://github.com/vvm3-IT/is117-brand-studio/blob/main/public/styleguide.html) were inspected November 17, 2026.

We reuse its colors, font families, calm voice, and generous spacing. We adapt the content and structure to campus exchange. The Quiet Reps training promise and logo remain associated with that separate project.

## Character and voice
Practical, welcoming, and clear. The campus brand acts like a helpful neighbor: useful information, plain language, and no inflated promises. This adapts the existing calm and encouraging voice to a different audience; audience fit remains untested.

## Two layout directions
**Photo-card marketplace:** image, title, price, condition, and status in a consistent card. Proposed for the later marketplace because appearance may help people identify relevant items.

**Text classifieds:** denser list with more items visible at once. Useful if students prioritize scanning many offers over item imagery.

Choose cards as the working direction; verify with actual students before claiming it performs better. For this documentation site, use a clear sidebar, local search, section outlines, readable tables, and separate pages for sources, assumptions, decisions, and contributions.

## Honest persuasion
Headline: **Buy and sell used essentials around campus.**

Supporting copy: Browse listings or give something you no longer need a new home.

- **Reciprocity:** provide useful condition and pickup guidance before asking someone to create a listing.
- **Commitment and consistency:** let a seller preview and correct a listing before publishing. Participation must remain voluntary; avoid preselected consent or hidden commitments.
- Use real listing prices and accurate item descriptions.
- Do not invent reviews, user counts, scarcity, savings, safety guarantees, or NJIT endorsement.
- A verification badge must describe a check actually performed. Email access does not prove current enrollment or trustworthy conduct.

## Planned design checks
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, phone-width layouts, explicit input labels, and status expressed in text. These are planned marketplace checks, not completed usability results. Documentation verification is tracked on the [verification page](./verification.md).
